# Free Parking — Declaration of Innovation Adaptation

**Atlanta, GA · Monthly**

When it becomes necessary to strengthen the community bonds, Free Parking gathers a small circle of founders to do the work that does not fit on a slide deck.

Members share North Star Metrics, peaks and valleys, and select one or two issues for deep dives each session. The declaration grounds how we treat introductions, confidentiality, and repair — the same values, applied to a room of seven founders instead of a room of thirty builders.

We come prepared, speak plainly, and leave having made someone else's problem smaller.

## Local Commitments

- What is shared in the room stays in the room. Confidentiality is the default, not the exception.
- We come to each session with our North Star Metrics prepared. Preparation is respect.
- We bring real problems, not polished ones. This is not a pitch room.
- Introductions from this group carry weight. We make them carefully and with consent.
- We do not recruit, sell, or solicit within the group.
- We repair harm directly. If something goes wrong between members, we address it.

## Contact

Free Parking is a private monthly peer circle for ATDC portfolio CEOs in Atlanta, GA.
